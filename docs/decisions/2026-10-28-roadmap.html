The 14 live checks, grouped (from ROADMAP.md)
  • Reads: Read mail again on the live board; a re-read without Gmail's change log (a week old); first read's progress inside the deployed board.
  • Apply and undo: move a labelled card, Review, Apply; filter status after a real Apply; Undo and Remove everything; new error toasts for a failed read or Apply.
  • Filters: drift and hand-back screens; the filters view on your mailbox; card actions (star, mark read, delete) read back as Labelboard's own.
  • Gmail tab and labels: ~25 rows a side, a merge and a drag merge, then undo; a fold, a leftover cleared, colours, then undo; label counts on ~180 labels.
  • On its own: Leaves the inbox after N days; Runs on its own reads every 6 h and applies only while nothing waits.
  • Install: INSTALL.md walked end to end on a fresh Google account.
Already decided against, and gaps kept on purpose (no decision needed)

  • ✖ Drop-on-card merge, undo through every Apply, undo taking labels off mail caught since.
